    Stat keeper key

    Needed once on each device to send plays.

    Stat keepers

    Both devices share this setting. With two keepers, each one sees only their team's players and plays.

    Teams and rosters

    Saving also keeps each team in Saved teams for next time. The team name is what the box score and scorebug show; Save as is the name it's kept under, so the boys and girls can both show as "FBCA" (Save as "FBCA Boys" and "FBCA Girls"). A player's name can be fixed mid-game; their plays stay with them.

    New game

    Clears every play for both keepers and the scorebug. Do this before tip-off, not between quarters.

    Past games

    A game is saved here, and on fbca.tv/boxscore under Past games, when the scorebug marks it final, and again when a new game starts. Download one as a spreadsheet, or delete a test game.
