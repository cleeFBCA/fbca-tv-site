        Defaults follow NFHS: four 8-minute quarters and 4-minute overtimes; 5 timeouts (three 60-second, two 30-second), plus one more for each team at the start of each overtime; two free throws starting with the 5th team foul of each quarter (no one-and-one), with fouls carrying from the 4th quarter into overtime; shot clock off, since TAPPS doesn't use one. If a game does, NFHS uses 35 seconds; the 20-second short reset is a common state option.

        BONUS shows on the team that shoots the free throws: the one whose opponent has reached the bonus foul number.
