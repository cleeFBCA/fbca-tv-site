    Player of the Game fan vote

    Fans vote at fbca.tv/vote, one vote per phone or computer. Pick the nominees, open voting, put up the QR lower third a few times, then close voting and show the results. This is the same vote the soccer page runs, so only one game can have a vote at a time.

      Defaults follow NFHS: four 8-minute quarters and 4-minute overtimes; 5 timeouts (three 60-second, two 30-second), plus one more for each team at the start of each overtime; two free throws starting with the 5th team foul of each quarter (no one-and-one), with fouls carrying from the 4th quarter into overtime; shot clock off, since TAPPS doesn't use one. If a game does, NFHS uses 35 seconds; the 20-second short reset is a common state option.

      BONUS shows on the team that shoots the free throws: the one whose opponent has reached the bonus foul number.

      • Space start / stop the clock
      • Q W E left team +1 / +2 / +3, A left −1
      • I O P right team +1 / +2 / +3, L right −1
      • S / K foul on the left / right team
      • R shot clock full reset, T short reset (when the shot clock is on)
      • X flip the possession arrow, H hide / show the bug
      • N show / hide the lower third in the form
      • V fan vote QR lower third, Esc take down the results

      Left and right follow the screen, so they stay under your hands after a swap.
